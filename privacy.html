Privacy notice

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: we keep what your club tags and the sign-in details needed to run the app, store it in the EU, never sell it, and delete it when you ask.

Who we are

Rock Solid Analytics, run by Tadhg Dornan, A63 AW20, Ireland, is responsible for the data described here. Contact: staff.rocksolidanalytics@gmail.com.

What we hold

  • Sign-in details: the email address of each coach, analyst or player who signs in, and their role in the club.
  • Club data: club name, county, crest and kit; squad names and positions; matches, tags, line-ups, notes, playlists and set plays; links to match videos (YouTube or other video links you add).
  • Team sheet photos: if you use the team sheet reader, the photo is sent to be read and is not kept afterwards.
  • How the app is used: a short record when the app is opened, a report is made, a tagger is opened or the Fan Zone is viewed (the club, the signed-in person if any, and the time), and the text of any error the app hits on your device. We use it only to see how the app is doing and to fix problems, keep it for 12 months, and never share it.
  • Payments: if you buy lifetime access, the club name and what the payer sends us. Card details go to Revolut, never to us.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, or any outside analytics service. The app keeps some data on your own device so tagging works without a signal.

Why we hold it

To run the app for your club (our agreement with your club), to take payment, and to answer you when you contact us. Player names are entered by the club, which decides what to record about its players. When we tag a game for a club (match reports or live tagging), we enter the names from the team sheet the club gives us, on the club’s behalf.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in, stored in the EU (Stockholm).
  • Anthropic: reads team sheet photos when you use that feature.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the app's web pages.
  • YouTube: plays the match videos you link.
  • Revolut: card payments.

Some of these companies are based outside the EU; they work under the EU's standard safeguards for such transfers. We never sell data or share it with anyone else.

Public links

If a club shares a Fan Zone link, anyone with the link can see that match's live score, team names, kits and scorers.

How long we keep it

For as long as the club uses the app. When a club or a person asks us to delete their data, we do so within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, by emailing us. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

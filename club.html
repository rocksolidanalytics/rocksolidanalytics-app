Match day

Tag the game as it happens

  • One tap for us, two for them. Every event goes to the right team without menus.
  • Pick the player by number. Scorers, wides and turnovers land on the right name.
  • Restarts tracked. Kickouts and puckouts, won or lost, and who won them.
  • Spots on the pitch. Mark where it happened live, after the match, or not at all.
  • Team sheet from a photo. Snap the sheet and the line-up fills itself, matched to your squad.

Stats and pitch map

See where the game was won and lost

  • Season and match dashboards. Attack, defence, restarts and players, for one game or the whole year.
  • Player breakdown. Scores, shots, tackles, restarts and turnovers for every player.
  • Expected scores. Bad shooting or bad shot choice? xS shows the quality of chances.
  • Pitch map. Every spot on one pitch, coloured by event or outcome.
  • Benchmark and conditions. Compare with other teams, and by referee, venue and weather.
Fan Zone and Live Stats Pad

Everyone follows the game, not just the tagger

  • Fan Zone. A live scoreboard anyone can open from a link. Share it in the club WhatsApp; no app or login needed.
  • Live Stats Pad. Management follow the numbers on their own phone while someone else tags.
  • Win probability and momentum. See the game swing, and get a flag after three scores in a row.
Opposition and coaching

Know who you are playing before you meet them

  • Opposition file. Every team you have met: results, scorers, shot map and your notes.
  • Scouting matches. Tag a rival's game from the stand and add it to their file.
  • Tactical pad. Drag jerseys on a pitch to explain a kickout or a defensive shape.
  • Player logins. Players join with the club code to see the stats and film for themselves.
